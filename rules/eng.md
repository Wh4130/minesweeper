> Minesweeper is back and better than ever! We’ve taken the retro vibes you love and added the brand-new :blue[**Mine Sweep**] and :blue[**Safe Tap**] features to boost the fun and your chances of victory!
> 
> - **The Battlefield:** The game features a **12 x 12 grid** with a total of 144 cells. Hidden beneath are **20 mines**, each placed with an equal probability across the board.
> - **The First Move:** Fortune favors the bold! Pick any cell to start. Since the first move is a leap of faith, you might hit a mine right away—if that happens, just shake it off and restart the game!
> - **What Happens Next?**
>   - :red[**Danger Nearby:**] If the uncovered cell is adjacent to mines, it will display a number showing how many mines are hiding in the surrounding 3x3 area.
>   - :green[**Clear Skies:**] If there are no mines nearby, the system will automatically scan **Up, Down, Left, and Right**. It will keep uncovering cells for you until it reaches a boundary where mines are present.
> - **Scoring:** Your score equals the number of safe cells uncovered. With 20 mines on the field, a **perfect score is 124**. Reach 124 to win automatically! Watch out—hitting a single mine ends the game instantly.
> - **Lucky Stars:** For every **10 points** earned, you receive a :orange[**Lucky Star**]. Spend your stars to trigger these game-changing abilities:
>   - :blue[**Mine Sweep**]: Consume one Lucky Star to randomly remove one mine from the map.
>   - :blue[**Safe Tap**]: Consume one Lucky Star to instantly uncover one guaranteed safe cell.
> - **Mark Your Path:** During the game, you can mark cells to strategize your moves. There are two types of markers to help you out: :orange[**Question Marks**] and :orange[**Flags**].
